# README media: shot list

The README has slots for one 30-second video and four screenshots (commented out at the top of `README.md`). Record
them, drop the files here with these names, then remove the `<!--` and `-->` lines around the media block.

GitHub shows an `.mp4` in the repo as a link, not a player. For an inline player, edit the README on github.com and
drag the video into the editor (GitHub hosts it and inserts a link); keep `trailer_poster.jpg` here either way.

## Settings for every capture

- 1920 x 1080, highest quality preset, HUD on unless noted, motion blur off for stills.
- Night, city map, a car that looks finished. No debug overlays, no browser UI (F11 full screen).
- Keep files small: stills as JPEG quality 85 (under 500 KB each), video H.264 MP4 under 10 MB (GitHub's limit for
  drag-and-drop on free accounts).

## Screenshots

| file | shot | why |
|---|---|---|
| `shot_city_night.jpg` | Chase camera, two or three cars side by side on a lit city street, mid-corner | the hook: what the game is |
| `shot_cockpit.jpg` | Cockpit or bonnet camera at high speed, speedometer visible | it is a driving sim |
| `shot_damage.jpg` | A car after a crash, dented panels, smoke if there is any, sharp focus | damage is a feature |
| `shot_telemetry.jpg` | Telemetry or tyre-temperature screen during a lap | the physics depth |

## 30-second video (`trailer.mp4` + `trailer_poster.jpg`)

| time | shot |
|---|---|
| 0-3 s | Title or the city skyline at night, engine starting |
| 3-10 s | Race start: lights, launch, three cars into the first corner |
| 10-17 s | Cockpit camera through a fast section, gear shifts audible |
| 17-22 s | A crash with visible damage |
| 22-27 s | A slide caught with countersteer (wheel or pad), tyre smoke |
| 27-30 s | Logo + "Play free in your browser" + the demo URL |

Poster frame: a still from 3-10 s. Record with OBS (free), cut with Shotcut or DaVinci Resolve (free).
