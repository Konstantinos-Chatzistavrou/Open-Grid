## Physics reference data
- **Powertrain reference set** (`data/powertrain/`): engine torque maps and gearboxes from Project Chrono vehicle
  JSON (BSD-3-Clause, commit `e166f3e5`, source files stored verbatim under `data/powertrain/source/`); final-drive
  ratios extracted from US EPA test-car certification CSVs (public domain, 17 USC §105). The Chrono maps are
  simplified reference data, not measured dyno curves.
- **Aerodynamic references** (`data/physics/aero_reference.json`): DrivAer Cd values from Elrefaie et al.,
  arXiv:2406.09624 (CC BY 4.0); NACA 0012 coefficients from Abbott & von Doenhoff, NACA Report 824 (public domain);
  Ahmed body 25° Cd cited from Ahmed (1984, SAE 840300) as a fact only.
- **Measured engine curve** (`data/physics/torque-reference.json`): Dolor & Temabor, "Design, Fabrication, and
  Performance Evaluation of a Locally Developed Automotive Engine Test Bed…", Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23856320,
  **CC BY 4.0** — one measured Toyota 7A-FE 1.8 L full-load curve, stored verbatim with the paper's own caveat.
- **CG and inertia reference** (`data/physics/inertia-reference.json`): measured values for the 1994/1996 Isuzu
  Trooper and 1996 Acura SLX published by NHTSA/VRTC in the Federal Register 62 FR 40611–40612 (29 July 1997),
  a US Government work in the public domain. Nine modern (2019-2021) measured vehicles come from the NHTSA 2020 NCAP
  Static Stability Factor report, docket NHTSA-2001-9663-0497, a US Government work in a federal docket (confirm
  contractor status before commercial redistribution); it publishes CG height and SSF, not moments of inertia.
- **Aerodynamic axle balance** (`data/physics/aero_reference.json`): Piechna, *Energies* 2022, 15, 5543,
  Table 4, **CC BY 4.0** — CFD/FSI values for a DrivAer-type covered-wheel car, explicitly not measured.
- **Braking envelope** (`data/physics/brakes-reference.json`): 49 CFR 571.135 (FMVSS 135), US Government work,
  public domain.
- **NACA 4-digit airfoil sections** (`data/physics/airfoils.json`): geometry from the published NACA definition in
  Abbott & von Doenhoff, *Theory of Wing Sections* (NACA Report 824, 1945), a US Government work in the public
  domain. Generated by `tools/physics/naca.mjs`; `cd0Estimate` values are estimates, not measurements.
- **Validation track geometry** (`data/validation/racetrack-database/`, Monza and Brands Hatch CSV):
  from TUMFTM/racetrack-database, © TUMFTM contributors, **GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0** (full text in that
  folder's `LICENSE`, provenance in `SOURCE.md`). Stored unmodified as separate data files; it is third-party
  material, **not** covered by this project's CC BY-NC 4.0 licence, and any redistribution must keep the LGPL notice
  and make the corresponding source available. It is used for validation only and is not linked into the engine.
- **CC BY 4.0 measurement datasets** (tyre tests on ice, four-post rig accelerations, vehicle-dynamics benchmark)
  are fetched on demand by `tools/physics/fetch-data.mjs` into a git-ignored directory, with URLs and sha256 in
  `data/physics/external/MANIFEST.json`; attribution and licence notes in `docs/physics-data.md`.
