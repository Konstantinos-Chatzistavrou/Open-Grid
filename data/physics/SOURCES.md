# Airfoil data

`airfoils.json` is generated by `tools/physics/naca.mjs` from the published NACA 4-digit
definition (Abbott & von Doenhoff, *Theory of Wing Sections*, NACA Report 824, 1945 - a US
Government work, public domain).

- Geometry: 61 cosine-spaced points per surface, upper and lower.
- `clAlphaPerRad`: thin-airfoil theory, 2*pi per radian (no finite-wing correction).
- `cd0Estimate`: **estimate**, 0.006 + 0.004*(t/0.12). Not measured; do not present it as test data.

Regenerate with:

```bash
node tools/physics/naca.mjs data/physics/airfoils.json
```
