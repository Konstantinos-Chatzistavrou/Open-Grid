Peak grip is the largest |F| / Fz at the nominal load over a slip sweep (to 1.0) with the file's limits off (≥ marks a peak at the end of the sweep: a lower bound); cornering stiffness is dFy/dalpha at zero slip. Provenance: measured and fitted-from-measurements need the measurement described; published-model is someone else's model parameters; synthetic describes no real tyre; unknown means the origin is not stated. Each downloaded file carries its provenance, licence and notice as comment lines at the top.
