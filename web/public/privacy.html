OPENGRID

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-27 · Open Grid (open-grid.racing)

Open Grid is a free, open-source, ad-free hobby racing simulator. We collect only the minimum data needed to save your profile and run optional online features.

1. Information We Collect

  • Guest play: You can play without signing in. Progress is saved locally in your browser.
  • Optional Sign-In (Google, GitHub, Discord, or Email): If you choose to sign in, we store your account identifier, email address, chosen driver username, cloud save data (garage/settings), and online racing stats (Driver Rating, Safety Rating, lap times, friends list).
  • Abuse prevention: A salted one-way hash of your network address is used temporarily for rate limiting and anti-cheat; raw IP addresses are not stored.

2. How We Use Information

Your account data is used strictly to authenticate you, sync your save across devices, and display leaderboards/multiplayer profiles. We run zero ads, use zero third-party ad trackers, and never sell or share personal data.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

Online account records are hosted on Cloudflare D1. You can sign out anytime from your Driver Profile, or request full account deletion via our GitHub repository or Discord server.

4. Open Source

The entire client and backend code are publicly auditable under Apache-2.0 at github.com/Konstantinos-Chatzistavrou/Open-Grid.
