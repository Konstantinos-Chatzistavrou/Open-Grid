Tyre Lab

Fit a tyre file to your measurements.

Pacejka Magic Formula 5.2 / 6.1 / 6.2, the same solver that is checked against MFeval on every commit, compiled to WebAssembly. It runs in this tab: nothing is uploaded.

Status: checked on synthetic data only (curves generated from known tyres, fitted from a different tyre and judged on points it never saw). Not yet shown on measured tyre data. A fit is only as good as the data.

1Your data

A table, one row per measured point. Columns: fz (N, positive) kappa alpha (rad, or alpha_deg) gamma fx fy mz; units in the header are honoured (SA [deg], P [kPa]). Signs as in the tyre file.

No data yet.

2Template tyre

Supplies the geometry, the nominal load and every coefficient your data cannot determine. Give your tyre's real size and nominal load.

Fit report

stagechannelrowscoefficientsrms error% of peakflags

Coefficients are not unique: many sets give nearly the same curves. The fit matches the curves, not necessarily the coefficients that generated them; never read a coefficient as a physical property.

Your data against the fit

Dots: your data. Solid: the fitted tyre. Dashed: the template before the fit. Each colour is one load; camber near zero.

What this is not

Not a manufacturer's tyre file: it describes your data, inside the range of load, slip and camber you measured. Not fitted: overturning moment, rolling resistance, turn slip, the aligning moment of combined slip, and anything your data did not cover (those coefficients keep the template's values and its licence). Source, tests and the car pipeline: docs/getting_started.md. Code Apache-2.0; template tyres are Project Chrono data (BSD-3).
